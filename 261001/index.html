Quick tips

Fun fact! — ask for the output format you want

Copilot doesn't only answer in chat — you can ask it to deliver the result as a ready-to-edit file in the format you need. Just name the format in your prompt and Copilot builds the document, deck, workbook or page for you.

Summarize this report as a Word document (.docx).
Turn these notes into a PowerPoint deck (.pptx).
Put these figures into an Excel spreadsheet (.xlsx).
Give me this as a single self-contained HTML page (.html).

Name the format and the structure you want — headings, a summary table, a 10-slide deck — and Copilot generates the file ready for you to refine.

Module 1 · Know yourself

Know your role & products

As a salesperson, you keep asking yourself these questions. Copilot can help you answer them.

Questions you keep asking yourself

  1. What is expected of me in this role — and how is success measured?
  2. What problem does each product solve, and for whom?
  3. How do I explain it simply — and what do I still not know?

How Copilot helps

  • Learning plan — job description + KPIs → 30-day plan
  • Product explainer — product sheet → plain language
  • Self-test — quizzes to find your gaps

Bring into the promptJob description · KPIs / targets · Product sheets · SOP excerpts (per BSI policy)

Ready-to-use prompts

Prompt 1 · Learning plan

I am a new [role, e.g. branch sales officer] at an Islamic bank branch, moving from a head-office role in [previous function]. Here are my job description and KPIs: [paste]. Build a 30-day learning plan: what to learn each week, who to ask, and how I know I am on track.

Prompt 2 · Product explainer

Explain this product to me as if I were a customer: [paste product sheet / attach file]. Give key benefits, target segment, how it works in simple words, and 5 likely customer questions with answers. Use only the information I provided and flag anything unclear.

Prompt 3 · Self-test quiz

Quiz me with 5 questions on [product name] based on [paste product sheet]. Ask one question at a time, wait for my answer, then give feedback and the correct answer.

Module 2 · Know your client

Know your client: profiling

As a salesperson, these are the questions you keep asking yourself. Copilot can assist you.

  • Who is this client & what do they do?
  • What’s happening in their market?
  • Who decides & what do they care about?
  • What might they need — and why now?

Ready-to-use prompts

Prompt 1 · Company & market research

Research public information about [company / industry] in [city], Indonesia: business overview, recent news, market trends, regulations and main competitors. Use bullet points and list your sources with links.

Prompt 2 · Role-play the decision maker

Act as the [role, e.g. finance manager] of a [type of business]. What are your top priorities and worries about banking and financing this year? What would make you trust a new bank salesperson?

Prompt 3 · Build the client profile

Fill this client-profile canvas: company snapshot, industry & market, decision maker, pain points, opportunity for us, open questions. Sources: [paste your meeting notes] + the research above. Mark fact vs. assumption.

M1-A · Role mastery plan — full master prompt

ROLE Act as a senior sales coach at an Indonesian Islamic (Sharia) bank branch. You help head-office staff succeed in their first branch sales role. GOAL Help me understand my new role quickly and build a practical plan to reach my targets. CONTEXT (pasted by me — use only this; do not invent bank policies, products or numbers) - Job description: [paste job description] - KPIs / targets: [paste KPIs/targets] - My background: [my background, e.g. years in head office, functions, sales experience] INSTRUCTIONS — work step by step 1. Role summary: explain my role in plain words (max 120 words): who my customers are, what I do day to day, and who I work with. 2. What success looks like: for each KPI, explain what it measures, why it matters to the branch, and which daily and weekly activities drive it. If a KPI is unclear, say so and suggest what to ask. 3. 30-60-90 day plan as a table: Period · Learning goals · Key activities · People to meet · Measurable milestone. 4. Skills gap: compare what the role requires with my background. List strengths I can reuse, gaps to close, and one concrete way to close each gap. 5. Top 10 questions to ask my branch manager, in priority order. 6. Weekly self-check routine: a checklist of max 8 items I can review every Friday. OUTPUT FORMAT Use headings 1–6 as above; tables where asked, bullet points elsewhere. Simple English, max 900 words. QUALITY RULES - Base everything on the material I pasted. Do not invent targets, figures, products or internal policies. - Mark any general assumption with "(assumption)". - End with a short list: "To confirm with my manager".

Per-product explainer, akad, FAQs and misconceptions, a comparison table and a quiz to test yourself.

ROLE Act as a product trainer for branch sales staff at an Indonesian Islamic (Sharia) bank. GOAL Help me master the products below so I can explain them simply and correctly to customers. CONTEXT (use ONLY this material)

~248 words

M1-B · Product mastery kit — full master prompt

ROLE Act as a product trainer for branch sales staff at an Indonesian Islamic (Sharia) bank. GOAL Help me master the products below so I can explain them simply and correctly to customers. CONTEXT (use ONLY this material) [paste product sheet(s)/SOP excerpt — or attach the file] INSTRUCTIONS — for EACH product in the material 1. A one-sentence explanation a customer would understand. 2. Sharia contract (akad) basis — only as stated in the material; if it is not there, write "not stated". 3. Target segments: who it is for. 4. Key benefits vs typical alternatives — keep comparisons generic; do not name or make claims about competitors unless the material does. 5. Eligibility and requirements (criteria, documents). 6. 5 frequently asked questions, each with a short answer. 7. 3 common misconceptions, each with the correct explanation. THEN 8. A comparison table across all products: Product · Akad · Target segment · Main benefit · Key requirement · Best used when. 9. A 5-question quiz to test me (mix multiple-choice and short answer). Put all answers at the very end under "Answer key". OUTPUT FORMAT One section per product with clear headings, then the comparison table, then the quiz. Simple English. QUALITY RULES - Use only the pasted material. Do not add rates, margins, fees, limits or features that are not in it. - Flag anything unclear, missing or contradictory as "TO CONFIRM: …". - Finish with the list of questions I should confirm with the product team.

~366 words

M2-A · Client deep profile + company structure tree — full master prompt

ROLE Act as a corporate research analyst supporting a relationship manager at an Indonesian Islamic (Sharia) bank. GOAL Build a deep, sourced profile of the client below — including its full group structure — so I can prepare for a first meeting. CLIENT - Company name: [company name] - City / region: [city/region] - Industry: [industry] SOURCES Search the web and use publicly available information only: the company website, annual and sustainability reports, IDX filings and disclosures (if listed), AHU / company-registry news, and reputable media. INSTRUCTIONS — work step by step 1. Company snapshot: legal name, year founded, headquarters, main business lines, size indicators (employees, revenue, assets — only if published), listing status. 2. Company structure tree (recursive): a. Identify the ultimate parent / group. b. Starting from the top, expand each level recursively: parent → subsidiaries → sub-subsidiaries, affiliates and joint ventures. c. For every entity show: ownership % (only where published), business line, location, and key leaders (commissioners / directors). d. Keep recursing until no further public information is found. e. Render it as an indented tree using ├── and └── with one entity per line. f. Tag every node with its source link and a confidence level: Confirmed / Reported / Unverified. 3. Business model and revenue drivers: how the group makes money, main customer types, key cost drivers. 4. Recent news and developments (last 12–24 months) as a dated list: expansion, investment, financing, management changes, regulation, disputes. 5. Likely banking needs per entity (e.g. working-capital or investment financing, cash management, payroll, trade finance, supply-chain financing). State the signal behind each need and label it a hypothesis. 6. Could not verify: list everything you could not confirm. OUTPUT FORMAT Headings 1–6. Put the tree in a code block. Add a numbered "Sources" list at the end and cite the source number after each fact, e.g. (S3). QUALITY RULES - Cite a source for every fact; if there is no source, do not present it as fact. - Never guess ownership or group relationships — mark them "Unverified" and explain why. - No personal data beyond the names and titles of publicly listed executives and commissioners. - Keep facts separate from your interpretation.

M2-B · Industry, market & decision-maker map — full master prompt

ROLE Act as an industry analyst and B2B sales strategist for an Indonesian Islamic (Sharia) bank. GOAL Help me understand my client's industry and the people who decide, so I can open the right conversations. CONTEXT - Industry: [industry] - Region: [region] - My meeting notes (anonymised — no nasabah names, account numbers, NIK or phone numbers): [paste my meeting notes (anonymised)] INSTRUCTIONS — work step by step 1. Industry overview and key trends in Indonesia, and in my region where data exists. 2. Regulations and policies affecting the industry, including the halal / Sharia angle where relevant. 3. Competitor landscape: the main players and how they compete (public information only). 4. Seasonality and cash-flow cycle: peak and low periods, typical payment terms, working-capital pressure points. 5. Stakeholder map as a table: Role · What they care about · Likely worries · How to open the conversation. Cover the roles usually involved in a banking decision (e.g. owner/director, finance, treasury, procurement, operations) plus any roles in my notes. 6. Needs hypotheses and "why now" triggers: which needs are likely, and which events make them urgent now. 7. 5 discovery questions per stakeholder — open, respectful and not leading. OUTPUT FORMAT Headings 1–7; the stakeholder map as a table; bullet points elsewhere. A numbered "Sources" list at the end. QUALITY RULES - Cite sources for market facts and figures; prefer recent data and state the year. - Label every assumption "(assumption)" and every point taken from my notes "(from notes)". - Do not invent client facts. If my notes are thin, list what I should find out at the next meeting.

M3-A · Pipeline & weekly report builder — full master prompt

ROLE Act as a sales-operations assistant for a branch sales officer at an Indonesian Islamic (Sharia) bank. GOAL Turn my raw prospect notes into a clean pipeline and a concise weekly report for my branch manager. CONTEXT (anonymised — prospects are coded, e.g. Prospect A; no nasabah names or IDs) - Prospect notes: [paste my prospect notes (anonymised)] - Pipeline stages used at my branch: [pipeline stages used at my branch] - Reporting period: [reporting period] - My targets for the period: [targets] - Report tone: [tone, e.g. formal and concise] INSTRUCTIONS — work step by step 1. Pipeline table with columns: Prospect · Segment · Need · Product fit · Stage · Est. value · Next step · Owner · Due date · Risk. Use only my stage names. 2. Prioritisation: rank the prospects and give a one-line reason for each (stage, urgency, fit, value if given). 3. Missing information: for each prospect, list what is still missing to move it forward. 4. Weekly report for my branch manager with these sections: Highlights · Progress vs targets · Blockers · Help needed · Next week plan. Max 250 words, in the tone above. OUTPUT FORMAT The table first, then numbered lists, then the report as plain text ready to paste into an email. QUALITY RULES - Do not invent values, dates, owners or stages — leave the cell blank if my notes do not give it. - Flag inconsistencies in my notes (e.g. a stage that does not match the next step). - Keep prospect codes exactly as I wrote them; add no personal data.

M3-B · Pitch & objection playbook — full master prompt

ROLE Act as an experienced Islamic-banking sales coach helping me prepare for a client meeting. GOAL Prepare a tailored pitch and objection-handling playbook for this meeting. CONTEXT (use only this material) - Client profile (from Module 2): [paste client profile from M2] - Product information (from Module 1): [paste product info from M1] - Meeting objective: [meeting objective] - Time available: [time available] INSTRUCTIONS — work step by step 1. Value proposition: 2–3 sentences linking the client's needs to the product's benefits. 2. 3-minute pitch script: Opener → Need → Solution → Proof → Ask. Use proof only from the pasted material; if there is none, write "no proof point provided". 3. 3 conversation openers, each from a different angle (industry trend, client news, a question). 4. Top 8 likely objections. For each: an empathetic acknowledgement, a simple Sharia-compliant response, and one follow-up question. 5. Closing and next-step options: 3 ways to ask for a concrete next step that fit the meeting objective and the time available. 6. A one-page follow-up message to send after the meeting — in Bahasa Indonesia and in English. OUTPUT FORMAT Headings 1–6. Show the objections as a table: Objection · Acknowledge · Response · Follow-up question. Plain, respectful language. QUALITY RULES - Do not state rates, margins, nisbah, fees, approval times or promises that are not approved and in the pasted material — use a [placeholder] for any figure. - Keep every statement Sharia-compliant and consistent with the akad described in the material. - No client personal data; refer to people by their role.

Modul 0 · Lebih Mengenal Microsoft Copilot!

Menulis prompt seperti pro

Prompt adalah cara Anda meminta Copilot melakukan sesuatu — membuat, merangkum, menyunting, mengubah, atau memberi Anda kabar terkini. Ajak bicara seperti asisten yang cakap: gunakan bahasa yang sederhana dan jelas, dengan konteks yang cukup agar hasilnya baik.

BuatRangkumSuntingUbahKabar terkiniPelajari

Empat bahan prompt

Prompt terbaik memuat empat hal — Tujuan, Konteks, Sumber, dan Ekspektasi.

Klik kartu bahan di atas untuk menambahkan bagiannya ke prompt di sini — klik sekali lagi untuk menghapusnya. Buatkan 3–5 poin untuk mempersiapkan pertemuan pertama saya dengan manajer keuangan [nama perusahaan nasabah] — saya sales officer baru di cabang BSI, dan mereka adalah usaha [industri] skala menengah yang berencana ekspansi. Gunakan catatan pertemuan yang saya tempel di bawah ini serta informasi publik tentang perusahaan dan industrinya. Tulis dengan bahasa sederhana, maksimal 20 kata per poin, dan akhiri dengan 3 pertanyaan yang sebaiknya saya ajukan.

Modul 1 · Kenali diri

Kenali peran & produk Anda

Sebagai tenaga sales, Anda terus menanyakan hal-hal ini pada diri sendiri. Copilot dapat membantu Anda menjawabnya.

Pertanyaan yang terus Anda tanyakan

  1. Apa yang diharapkan dari saya di peran ini — dan bagaimana keberhasilan diukur?
  2. Masalah apa yang diselesaikan setiap produk, dan untuk siapa?
  3. Bagaimana menjelaskannya dengan sederhana — dan apa yang belum saya ketahui?

Bagaimana Copilot membantu

  • Rencana belajar — deskripsi pekerjaan + KPI → rencana 30 hari
  • Penjelas produk — lembar produk → bahasa sederhana
  • Uji diri — kuis untuk menemukan celah pengetahuan Anda

Bawa ke dalam promptDeskripsi pekerjaan · KPI / target · Lembar produk · Kutipan SOP (sesuai kebijakan BSI)

Prompt siap pakai

Prompt 1 · Rencana belajar

Saya adalah [peran, mis. branch sales officer] baru di sebuah kantor cabang bank syariah, pindah dari posisi di kantor pusat pada fungsi [fungsi sebelumnya]. Berikut deskripsi pekerjaan dan KPI saya: [tempel di sini]. Susun rencana belajar 30 hari: apa yang dipelajari setiap minggu, kepada siapa bertanya, dan bagaimana saya tahu sudah berada di jalur yang benar.

Prompt 2 · Penjelas produk

Jelaskan produk ini kepada saya seolah-olah saya nasabah: [tempel lembar produk / lampirkan file]. Sampaikan manfaat utama, segmen sasaran, cara kerjanya dengan bahasa sederhana, serta 5 pertanyaan nasabah yang mungkin muncul beserta jawabannya. Gunakan hanya informasi yang saya berikan dan tandai hal yang belum jelas.

Prompt 3 · Kuis uji diri

Uji saya dengan 5 pertanyaan tentang [nama produk] berdasarkan [tempel lembar produk]. Ajukan satu pertanyaan setiap kali, tunggu jawaban saya, lalu berikan umpan balik dan jawaban yang benar.

PERAN Bertindaklah sebagai coach sales senior di kantor cabang bank syariah di Indonesia. Anda membantu staf kantor pusat agar berhasil di peran sales cabang pertama mereka. TUJUAN Bantu saya memahami peran baru saya dengan cepat dan menyusun rencana praktis untuk mencapai target saya. KONTEKS (saya tempelkan — gunakan hanya ini; jangan mengarang kebijakan, produk, atau angka bank) - Deskripsi pekerjaan: [tempel deskripsi pekerjaan] - KPI / target: [tempel KPI/target] - Latar belakang saya: [latar belakang saya, mis. lama bekerja di kantor pusat, fungsi, pengalaman sales] LANGKAH — kerjakan tahap demi tahap 1. Ringkasan peran: jelaskan peran saya dengan bahasa sederhana (maks. 120 kata): siapa nasabah saya, apa yang saya kerjakan sehari-hari, dan dengan siapa saya bekerja. 2. Seperti apa keberhasilan itu: untuk setiap KPI, jelaskan apa yang diukur, mengapa KPI itu penting bagi cabang, serta aktivitas harian dan mingguan apa yang mendorongnya. Jika ada KPI yang kurang jelas, sampaikan dan sarankan apa yang perlu ditanyakan. 3. Rencana 30-60-90 hari dalam bentuk tabel: Periode · Target belajar · Aktivitas utama · Orang yang perlu ditemui · Tonggak capaian yang terukur. 4. Kesenjangan keterampilan: bandingkan kebutuhan peran dengan latar belakang saya. Sebutkan kekuatan yang bisa saya manfaatkan kembali, kesenjangan yang perlu ditutup, dan satu cara konkret untuk menutup setiap kesenjangan. 5. 10 pertanyaan teratas untuk kepala cabang saya, diurutkan berdasarkan prioritas. 6. Rutinitas evaluasi diri mingguan: daftar periksa maks. 8 butir yang bisa saya tinjau setiap hari Jumat. FORMAT OUTPUT Gunakan judul 1–6 seperti di atas; tabel jika diminta, poin-poin di bagian lain. Bahasa Indonesia yang sederhana, maks. 900 kata. ATURAN KUALITAS - Dasarkan semuanya pada materi yang saya tempelkan. Jangan mengarang target, angka, produk, atau kebijakan internal. - Tandai setiap asumsi umum dengan "(asumsi)". - Akhiri dengan daftar singkat: "Perlu dikonfirmasi dengan atasan saya".

M2-A · Profil mendalam nasabah + pohon struktur perusahaan — master prompt lengkap

PERAN Bertindaklah sebagai analis riset korporasi yang mendukung seorang relationship manager di bank syariah di Indonesia. TUJUAN Susun profil yang mendalam dan bersumber tentang nasabah di bawah ini — termasuk struktur grupnya secara lengkap — agar saya siap untuk pertemuan pertama. NASABAH - Nama perusahaan: [nama perusahaan] - Kota / wilayah: [kota/wilayah] - Industri: [industri] SUMBER Cari di web dan gunakan hanya informasi yang tersedia untuk publik: situs web perusahaan, laporan tahunan dan laporan keberlanjutan, laporan dan keterbukaan informasi di BEI/IDX (jika tercatat di bursa), berita AHU / registri perusahaan, serta media yang kredibel. LANGKAH — kerjakan tahap demi tahap 1. Gambaran perusahaan: nama resmi, tahun berdiri, kantor pusat, lini bisnis utama, indikator skala (jumlah karyawan, pendapatan, aset — hanya jika dipublikasikan), status pencatatan di bursa. 2. Pohon struktur perusahaan (rekursif): a. Identifikasi induk tertinggi / grup (ultimate parent). b. Mulai dari puncak, uraikan setiap tingkat secara rekursif: induk → anak perusahaan → cucu perusahaan, perusahaan afiliasi, dan joint venture. c. Untuk setiap entitas, tampilkan: % kepemilikan (hanya jika dipublikasikan), lini bisnis, lokasi, dan pimpinan kunci (komisaris / direksi). d. Terus lakukan rekursi hingga tidak ada lagi informasi publik yang ditemukan. e. Sajikan sebagai pohon berindentasi menggunakan ├── dan └── dengan satu entitas per baris. f. Beri setiap node tautan sumber dan tingkat keyakinan: Terkonfirmasi / Dilaporkan / Belum terverifikasi. 3. Model bisnis dan pendorong pendapatan: bagaimana grup menghasilkan pendapatan, jenis pelanggan utama, pendorong biaya utama. 4. Berita dan perkembangan terbaru (12–24 bulan terakhir) dalam daftar bertanggal: ekspansi, investasi, pembiayaan, pergantian manajemen, regulasi, sengketa. 5. Kemungkinan kebutuhan perbankan per entitas (mis. pembiayaan modal kerja atau investasi, cash management, payroll, trade finance, supply chain financing). Sebutkan sinyal di balik setiap kebutuhan dan beri label hipotesis. 6. Tidak dapat diverifikasi: daftar semua hal yang tidak dapat Anda konfirmasi. FORMAT OUTPUT Judul 1–6. Letakkan pohon struktur dalam code block. Tambahkan daftar "Sumber" bernomor di bagian akhir dan cantumkan nomor sumber setelah setiap fakta, mis. (S3). ATURAN KUALITAS - Cantumkan sumber untuk setiap fakta; jika tidak ada sumbernya, jangan sajikan sebagai fakta. - Jangan pernah menebak kepemilikan atau hubungan grup — tandai "Belum terverifikasi" dan jelaskan alasannya. - Tanpa data pribadi, selain nama dan jabatan direksi dan komisaris yang diumumkan secara publik. - Pisahkan fakta dari interpretasi Anda.

Jika catatan saya minim, sebutkan apa yang perlu saya cari tahu pada pertemuan berikutnya.

M3-B · Playbook pitch & penanganan keberatan — master prompt lengkap

PERAN Bertindaklah sebagai coach sales perbankan syariah berpengalaman yang membantu saya mempersiapkan pertemuan dengan nasabah. TUJUAN Siapkan pitch dan playbook penanganan keberatan yang disesuaikan untuk pertemuan ini. KONTEKS (gunakan hanya materi ini) - Profil nasabah (dari Modul 2): [tempel profil nasabah dari M2] - Informasi produk (dari Modul 1): [tempel info produk dari M1] - Tujuan pertemuan: [tujuan pertemuan] - Waktu yang tersedia: [waktu yang tersedia] LANGKAH — kerjakan tahap demi tahap 1. Untuk masing-masing: pengakuan yang empatik, respons sederhana yang sesuai syariah, dan satu pertanyaan lanjutan. 5.
